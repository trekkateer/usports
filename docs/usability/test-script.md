# Usability Test: Usports

Fall 2026

## Introduction

Thank you for agreeing to test Usports. Usports is a mobile app for intramural sports matchmaking at Calvin. The idea is that you shouldn't have to spam group chats or have to join an intramural club to find a pickup game. You can create or join games based on **location, time, difficulty, and skill level**. Anyone can host a game, and anyone can join one that has open spots.

We're testing the app, not you. You can't do anything wrong here. If something is confusing, that's a problem with the app, and that's exactly what we want to find. Please be honest, and don't worry about hurting our feelings.

For the test, I'll ask you to do some tasks, and I'd like you to **think out loud** while you do them. Tell me what you're looking at, what you expect to happen, what you're unsure about, and what you're tempted to tap. If you get stuck, I won't be able to help, but that's useful information too. This is a prototype, so some things may not work yet.

I'll record the screen and your comments, but they won't be made public. Only aggregate findings will be shared. If you'd like to stop at any point, just say so.

## Background Questions

We assume you're a Calvin student who has used a phone app to organize or join social activities.

1. What sports do you play, and how often?
2. How do you currently find pickup or intramural games? (Group chats, friends, posters, intramural sign-ups?)
3. Have you ever wanted to play but couldn't find a game, a group, or enough people? What happened?
4. Have you used any apps to join events or games before (e.g., Meetup, Strava, GroupMe)? What did you like or dislike?
5. When you join a game, what do you care about most (time, location, skill level, who else is going)?

Before we begin, do you have any questions for me?

## The Test

*Give the participant this scenario:* "You're a Calvin student who loves basketball and wants to play more pickup games on campus. You've just heard about Usports and are trying it for the first time."

**Task 1: Get started.** Create an account using your Calvin email.

**Task 2: Make yourself known.** Set up your profile so other players know what you play and how experienced you are.

**Task 3: Find a game.** Find a basketball game happening tomorrow.

**Task 4: Check the roster.** Figure out whether that game still needs players.

**Task 5: Try it out.** Join a game, then change your mind and leave it.

**Task 6: Be the organizer.** You want to get a group together. Host a game that needs at least 6 players.

### Final observations

- What's your overall impression of the app?
- Was anything confusing, missing, or surprising?
- Would you use this to find games? Why or why not?
- If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## Thank You

Thank you so much for your time and honesty. You've helped us find problems we couldn't have spotted on our own, and your feedback will directly shape how Usports gets built. We'll keep your session private and only share aggregate results. If you'd like a summary of what we learned, we'd be glad to send one.

## Facilitator Notes (not read aloud)

- Read the tasks verbatim and never name buttons, tabs, or screens.
- If a participant asks "Should I tap X?", reply "What would you do if I weren't here?"
- Note where they hesitate, backtrack, or misread labels, and time each task.
- Mark each task as completed, completed with difficulty, or not completed.
- Give the host task a concrete detail if asked (e.g., basketball, tomorrow evening, any location they choose).
- Pilot the script with one person before Thanksgiving break and revise any wording that leads them.
